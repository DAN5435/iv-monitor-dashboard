# Sarawak Alternative Rural Electrification Scheme (SARES) - Key Points

## Project Overview
- **Goal**: Full electrification of Sarawak by 2025 (ahead of UN's 2030 target)
- **Investment**: Over RM800 Million (Federal and State funding)
- **Coverage**: 546 villages, 15,000+ families, 54,067+ individuals (as of May 2023)

## Technical Specifications

### Power Capacity Per Household
- 1kW power capacity
- 3 kWh daily energy allocation
- 2-day battery backup (6 kWh storage)
- Energy limiters for consumption monitoring

### System Types
1. **Centralised Solar Power System (CSPS)**
   - Main system type for most villages
   - Central solar farm and powerhouse
   - Metal-clad construction for easy transport

2. **Solar Home System (SHS)**
   - For isolated individual houses
   - Individual solar panels, inverter, battery bank
   - Self-contained units

## Geographical Coverage
- **Main Focus**: Kapit Division (>50% of projects)
- **Other Areas**:
  - Miri Division
  - Bintulu
  - Sri Aman
  - Sarikei
  - Limbang

## Benefits

### Economic Impact
- Monthly savings: RM300-400 per household on fuel
- New income opportunities from night activities
- Local employment during construction
- Reduced travel costs for supplies

### Community Benefits
- 24/7 electricity access
- Food preservation capability
- Better education opportunities
- Access to information and entertainment
- Community ownership of systems

## Implementation Strategy
1. **Community Involvement**
   - Local labor hiring
   - Material supply opportunities
   - Transportation services

2. **Training & Support**
   - Operation and maintenance training
   - Electrical safety education
   - Ongoing technical support

## Future Plans
- Battery replacement programs
- System upgrades for growing villages
- Future grid integration where possible
- Continuous technical support

## Success Factors
1. **Strong Government Support**
2. **Quality Technical Design**
3. **Community Engagement**
4. **Sustainable Implementation Model**
